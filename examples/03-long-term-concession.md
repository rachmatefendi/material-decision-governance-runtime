# Example 03 — Long-Term Concession

> **Synthetic illustration — subordinate to [SPECIFICATION §12](../SPECIFICATION.md#12-governance-outcomes-and-resolution-order).** [§13](../SPECIFICATION.md#13-canonical-verdict-contract) defines the canonical object; this example does not define a competing contract.

## Request

“Approve a long-term infrastructure concession requiring substantial upfront capital, with repayment dependent on future revenue sharing.”

## Failure discovery

Potential binding concerns:

- demand is not contractually committed;
- construction overrun is uncapped;
- termination compensation is undefined.

Monitored concerns may include:

- technology obsolescence;
- operating-cost inflation.

## Governance implication

A large project does not automatically require rejection.

The structure may become governable if the non-survivable exposures are converted into enforceable conditions.

## Conditional target verdict

This is a possible later outcome, not a verdict established by the request. It applies only when required authority is satisfied and no higher-precedence `block`, `hold`, or `escalate` condition under §12 applies.

```text
permit_with_conditions
```

## Example binding conditions

```text
Verified minimum revenue or equivalent availability support
Capped sponsor equity exposure
Independent cost verification and contingency
Termination compensation or step-in rights
Revalidation at financial close
```

If a binding condition fails, re-evaluate the current state using §12; do not continue under the earlier conditional verdict.
