# Example 02 — Capital Allocation

> **Synthetic illustration — subordinate to [SPECIFICATION §12](../SPECIFICATION.md#12-governance-outcomes-and-resolution-order).** [§13](../SPECIFICATION.md#13-canonical-verdict-contract) defines the canonical object; this example does not define a competing contract.

## Request

“Approve deploying third-party capital into a leveraged product with attractive projected returns and low expected drawdown.”

## Ordinary reasoning might focus on

- expected return;
- historical volatility;
- portfolio fit;
- scenario analysis.

## MDGR failure discovery

A credible path to total loss is identified under a regime outside the stated assumptions.

Additional issue:

- required risk authority has not approved the exposure.

## Governance implication

Attractive expected return does not cancel:

- an unmitigated absorbing downside path;
- missing required authority.

## Illustrative verdict

```text
BLOCK
```

The decision may later be reconsidered if the structure itself changes enough to remove the binding failure and the required authority is obtained. That would be a new governed state, not a rhetorical override of the original finding.
